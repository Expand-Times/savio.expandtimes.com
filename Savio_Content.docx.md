Permissions and Privacy

Accessibility Service: Savio uses it to detect when you open apps you choose to limit or block, and to recognise when a Shorts or Reels screen is playing so it can block it. Long-form videos and other content are not blocked. Savio does not read typed text, messages, or passwords, and does not collect or share what appears on your screen.

VPN Service (Safe DNS): If you turn on adult content blocking, Savio asks you to approve Android's VPN permission. It uses a local VPN to filter DNS requests and block adult and harmful sites.

Foreground Service: Savio runs a foreground service while a screen time timer, focus timer, Pomodoro timer, or app block is active to keep the timer accurate and show a manageable notification.

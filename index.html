Transparency & Security

Permissions and Privacy

Savio is designed with user privacy first. We never harvest your personal data. Here is exactly how platform permissions work:

Accessibility Service

Savio uses it to detect when you open apps you choose to limit or block, and to recognise when a Shorts or Reels screen is playing so it can block it.

✓ Long-form videos and other content are not blocked.
✓ Savio does NOT read typed text, messages, or passwords.
✓ Does NOT collect or share screen content.

VPN Service (Safe DNS)

If you turn on adult content blocking, Savio asks you to approve Android's VPN permission.

✓ Uses a 100% local on-device VPN solely to filter DNS requests.
✓ Blocks adult and harmful sites instantly.
✓ Does not route traffic through third-party servers.

Foreground Service

Savio runs a foreground service while a screen time timer, focus timer, Pomodoro timer, or app block is active.

✓ Keeps your focus and study timers 100% accurate.
✓ Displays a convenient notification to pause or resume sessions.
✓ Shuts down as soon as timers complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Savio — your AI focus coach and smart app blocker.

1
How does Savio block Reels & Shorts?

Savio's Reels blocker and Shorts blocking detect when a short-video feed opens and stop it right there. Long-form videos and everything else in the same apps play as normal.

2
Does Savio read my private messages?

No, never. Savio does not read typed text, messages, or passwords, and does not collect or share what appears on your screen. Your privacy is 100% protected.

3
What is the AI Focus Coach?

Savio guides you with daily reflections and personalized tips, helping you recognize triggers and build steady habits so beating phone addiction feels like progress, not punishment.

What are No Phone sessions?

Savio is a free Focus App with an optional Pro subscription for advanced tools like strict mode. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime directly in Google Play.

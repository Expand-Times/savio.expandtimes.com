FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Savio?

Savio is a screen time and focus app with a personal AI focus coach. It blocks distracting apps and addictive Reels and Shorts, and helps you build healthier digital habits.

Can I block just the short-form feeds?

Yes. Turn on blocking for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight, so you can still use the rest of each app.

What is strict mode?

Strict mode helps you stick to your blocks. Once your session or challenge is on, Savio keeps your distracting apps and feeds blocked until you're done.

Can I still get important notifications?

Yes. Use the notification whitelist to allow the apps that matter, and review anything that was blocked in one place.

Does Savio block adult websites?

Yes. Control Plus lets you restrict your browser from opening adult websites, and you can add your own distracting sites to the block list.

Where can I get Savio?

Savio is available on Google Play for Android.

Privacy Policy for Savio: Screen Time & Focus AI

App Name: Savio: Screen Time & Focus AI

Tagline: Save your time

Developer: Expand Times

Official Document: Savio Privacy Policy Google Document

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Core Privacy Principle: Savio is designed with your digital wellbeing and privacy as our highest priority. Savio never reads typed text, private messages, or passwords, and does not collect or sell what appears on your screen.

1. Overview and Core Purpose

Savio is an AI focus coach and Focus App created to help users stop doomscrolling, cut screen time, block Reels & Shorts, and regain productivity. To provide intelligent blocking, study timers, and digital detox assistance, Savio uses standard Android platform services strictly for local functionality.

2. Permissions and Platform Services

Savio utilizes specific device permissions solely to deliver user-requested focus and blocking features:

Accessibility Service

Why Savio uses it: Savio uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when you open apps that you have chosen to limit or block, and to recognise when a short-video screen (such as Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts) is playing so it can block the feed immediately.

  • Selective Blocking: Long-form videos, search, and other features in the same apps are not blocked.
  • Zero Content Extraction: Savio does NOT read typed text, personal messages, keystrokes, or passwords.
  • No Screen Recording or Sharing: Savio does not collect, record, or share what appears on your screen.

VPN Service (On-Device DNS Content Filtering)

Why Savio uses it: When you enable the adult content filter ("Block Nud1ty" feature) under Controls, Savio requests your explicit permission to establish Android's VpnService.

  • Local On-Device Filtering: Savio sets up a local on-device loopback tunnel interface strictly to capture and filter Domain Name System (DNS) queries on UDP port 53.
  • Family-Safe Resolver: DNS lookups are routed directly to trusted, privacy-focused Cloudflare for Families resolvers (1.1.1.3 / 1.0.0.3), which automatically block known adult, explicit, and malware domains directly at the DNS level.
  • No Remote Proxy or General Traffic Routing: Savio does NOT route your general internet traffic, web browsing, application data, photos, videos, or messages through any remote proxy or external VPN servers. All non-DNS network traffic flows directly through your normal network connection completely untouched.
  • Strict Zero-Logging & Privacy: Savio does NOT inspect, collect, monitor, track, log, or store your browsing history, visited URLs, web searches, or DNS queries. The filtering process operates entirely in real time without recording your activity.
  • User Control & Transparency: The service is only initiated at your request after prominent disclosure, displays an ongoing persistent notification while active, and can be turned off at any time from within the app or directly via the "Turn Off" button on the notification.

Foreground Service

Why Savio uses it: Savio runs an active foreground service while protection or timed focus features are engaged (such as real-time DNS adult content blocking via specialUse, screen time limits, focus timers, Pomodoro sessions, or app blocking).

  • Uninterrupted Protection & Precision: Background tasks and deferred workers (like WorkManager) can be delayed or terminated by Android battery optimizations and Doze mode, which would cause filtering or active focus blocks to fail. Running as a foreground service guarantees continuous, real-time protection and accurate timing while you use your device.
  • Ongoing Status & Instant Controls: While active, Android displays a persistent notification in your notification shade (e.g., "Savio Nudity Blocker Active"), ensuring full transparency and providing one-tap controls to pause, manage, or instantly turn off the service.

3. Information We Collect and Process

Savio operates with privacy-by-design:

  • Usage Statistics & Streaks: Data regarding your focus streaks, timer completion, and app usage limits are stored locally on your device.
  • AI Focus Coach Reflections: Daily reflection prompts and personalized focus tips are processed to support your habit formation without transmitting your sensitive private files.
  • No Personal Data Sale: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security

All configuration data, timer settings, blocklists, and streak stats are stored locally on your device using standard Android security measures. We implement appropriate technical safeguards to ensure local data integrity.

5. Children’s Privacy

Savio is intended for students and general audiences seeking better focus and time management. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
